## Methodology

- **Today's runs** (all identical config, all 59 OK / 0 hpcx fail):
  - Run 1: `20260425_111753` (jobs 84939–85009)
  - Run 2: `20260425_115950` (jobs 85010–85080)

## Interpretation

### Regressions (3 — `all_reduce ring 4n`)

Real, repeatable, **isolated to a single algorithm × scale combination**. Ring at 4 nodes lost ~6% across all message sizes (-5.2 to -6.4%). Diagnosis suggestions (out of scope for this run):

- Could be a NCCL ring-construction change between 4/20 and now (cluster nodes may have different intra-node topology after a reboot).
- Could be that a specific node now in the 4n allocation has degraded one HCA (we ran with no exclude — could re-run with `--exclude` of suspect nodes to test).
- **Production impact: zero.** NCCL never selects ring at 4n for all_reduce — CollNet SHARP wins by 24% (384.4 vs 311.9). The user-visible perf path is unaffected.

### Improvements (7, dominated by `nvls_tree`)

Real, repeatable, **+5-9% gain on all `nvls_tree` at 4-32 nodes**. NVLSTree was below ring/CollNet SHARP at 4/20 (~266 GB/s) and now sits ~280-290 GB/s. Likely a NCCL parameter or topology-detection change. **Still not the best algorithm at any of these scales** — CollNet SHARP remains dominant.

### Bottom line

- **Cluster fabric is performance-stable vs 4/20.** All production-relevant headline numbers are within ±0.5%.
- **One real -6% regression** (ring 4n all_reduce) — unactionable because production doesn't use that algorithm there.
- **One real +8% improvement cluster** (nvls_tree 4-32n) — also unactionable but a positive sign.
- **No fabric degradation. No action required from perf side.**
