## Three takeaways

1. **Cluster is reproducible.** 13 of 15 configs within ±2% of 04-20, 11 within ±1%. Aggregate ΣBench essentially unchanged (+0.85% vs 04-20). The same official configs deliver the same numbers — no cluster-wide regression and no fabric degradation across the workload suite.

2. **One real improvement: Qwen3 235B BF16 +10.2%** (605 → 667). 9 steady-state samples within 0.4% of each other → real signal. Likely picking up MB/26.02 stack improvements similar to those that benefited 70B NVFP4 in the 04-22 rebench. Other MoE configs (DSV3 BF16, Qwen3 235B FP8) didn't move similarly, so this may be Qwen3-BF16-specific. **Open question:** is this latent headroom that other configs could also exploit?

3. **One real regression: Llama 405B NVFP4 −7.2%** (1800 → 1670, confirmed by 2 runs). NVFP4 is the most bandwidth-sensitive dtype, and PP=16 makes this the most cross-node-comm-heavy of all 15 configs — exactly where this morning's per-group NCCL scan (`~/johnson/worklog/2026-04-26_nccl_per_group_scaling.md`) flagged 20 fabric-weak nodes (12 4n-slow + 8 inter-quad-slow). Today's run included all 64 idle nodes; 04-20 used a 9-node historical exclude `[130,190,197,199,201,211,228,233,239]`. Hypothesis: the fabric weakness only matters at full-cluster scale on the most BW-sensitive workload. Would be confirmed by a 3rd run with 04-20's exclude.

## Infrastructure quirks logged today

- **submit_session_B.sh has a stale `--exclude=[181,190]`** → leaves 62 usable nodes, 64-node jobs perma-PD. Pre-emptive cancel + sed-fix to drained `141` after each submit (no-op exclude).
- **Don't quote glob paths in `ls -t`** — `ls -t "$dir/pretrain_*"` passes literal `*`. Use unquoted.
- **Watchdog grep needs literal backticks** for `` `Trainer.fit` stopped: `max_steps=10` reached.``. Use `grep -F`.
- **Direct sbatch (vs llmb-run) needs:** `#SBATCH --chdir=/tmp` + `export HOME=/tmp` + `export NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME=bond0`. llmb-run sets these implicitly.
- **Nemotron4 340B post-srun stalls 3-5 min** after `Trainer.fit stopped` — must scancel to release nodes.
