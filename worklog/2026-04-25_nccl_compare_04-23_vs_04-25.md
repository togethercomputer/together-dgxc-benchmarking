## 7. Bottom line

1. **Fabric is healthy.** Every algorithm × scale point overlapping between the two runs is within ±5% on production paths; CollNet SHARP at every scale is within ±1.1%.
2. **The 04-23 hpcx blocker is fully worked around** by local-disk staging. 64-node tests now run reliably without the host `/opt/hpcx` reinstall.
3. **One isolated regression survives:** `all_reduce ring 4n` -6%. Confirmed real (3 runs, CV 0.4%) but production-irrelevant; NCCL never selects ring at 4n because CollNet SHARP is +24% on the same path.
4. **64-node fabric numbers (first since 04-09):** CollNet SHARP all_reduce 363 GB/s, all_gather 362 GB/s — clean baseline going forward. NVLS collapses to 278 GB/s at 64n; do not force `NCCL_NVLS_ENABLE=1` for ≥64-node training.
5. **DSV3 BF16 512-GPU hang is not explained by the fabric.** Bare-host NCCL works at 64n. Remaining hypothesis is the in-container NCCL 2.28.9 + Pyxis stack — next step is the in-container NCCL run that 04-23 recommended.
